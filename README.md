# Leo UK AI Playbook for Account Management
 This playbook is a practical guide to where AI fits into modern Account Management. It focuses on the work AMs actually do every day: understanding briefs, building plans, managing projects, creating client communications, strengthening recommendations, uncovering opportunities and driving growth.
 
The goal is simple: help teams spend less time on administration and more time on thinking, relationships and adding value. AI should accelerate the work, not replace judgement. Human ownership, commercial thinking and client understanding remain at the heart of everything we do.
